## North-star role

Persistent discovery currently finds a market pair first and analyzes its
causal premises later. Exact semantic admission prevents literal repetition,
but the search substrate remains dominated by lexical neighborhoods and
preselected relation families. This misses the main reusable idea in soft
semantic arbitrage: different market predicates may depend on the same latent
world state even when their words and settlement outcomes differ.

For example, an August shooting market and a September public-livestream market
can be linked through survival, health, availability, and personal-performance
states. The link is not hard mutual exclusion and carries no naked probability.
It is a mechanism-shaped reason to search, enumerate adverse counter-scenarios,
and decide how much estimation error the displayed price gap can tolerate.

## Ontology decision

A world-state mechanism route is an evidence-bound, directional search
hypothesis with four distinct roles:

1. **subject** — the person, organization, asset, office, team, or other world
   object whose state is relevant;
2. **trigger event** — a bounded proposition that may change a named state;
3. **affected state** — a small typed state dimension such as existence,
   incumbency, physical capability, legal eligibility, operational
   availability, possession, location, or willingness;
4. **dependent event** — a bounded proposition whose occurrence may require or
   be influenced by that state in a later or overlapping interval.

Mechanism direction and temporal posture are identity-bearing. Agent prose,
confidence, run identity, and model do not define the route. Counter-scenarios
are mandatory: non-fatal recovery, proxy/recorded participation, changed
settlement wording, and unrelated subject identity must remain visible rather
than being collapsed into a false hard relation.

The route has `WORLD_STATE_SEARCH_ROUTING_ONLY` authority. It cannot assert the
mechanism is true, assign epsilon, alter feasible settlement states, publish a
certificate, dispatch itself, or move value. Independent semantic review and
the existing adverse-state probability/calibration pipeline retain those
boundaries.

  dependent roles; never wake on subject-name membership alone.
- [x] Require time-compatible cross-role pairs, cap membership/fanout, and

- [x] Materialize one non-recursive relation-discovery work item per canonical

- [x] Seed `CONDITIONAL`, `CONFLICTING`, `IMPLIES`, and
  `MUTUALLY_EXCLUSIVE` only as candidate relation kinds; the route itself does
  not choose the semantic conclusion.
- [x] Reuse ordinary campaign authority, novelty admission, semantic review,
  probability estimation, calibration, economics, and opportunity gates.

The observation slice uses schema 51 and a provider-free role matcher over
reviewed subject labels, bounded ontology predicate families, and temporal
posture. Startup and catalog-refresh reconciliation persist exact observations
before wakes; retained wakes enter the ordinary relation-work projection but
cannot activate a campaign. First observation and first subject approval form a
baseline, unchanged replay stays quiet, and empty catalog refreshes are treated
as transport absence rather than evidence that a world state disappeared.

## Qualification gates

- [x] A shooting/public-livestream fixture retains a directional physical-
  capability mechanism plus a non-fatal-recovery counter-scenario.
- [x] A later public-appearance listing wakes the mechanism without a predefined
  claim or exact shared phrase beyond the reviewed subject binding.
- [x] Reversing trigger/dependent roles, changing the state dimension, or
  changing time posture rotates identity.
- [x] A proxy/recorded-appearance case remains an adverse counter-scenario and
  prevents the route from becoming a hard relation.
- [x] An ambiguous same-name subject, broad celebrity cluster, or title-only
  lexical match cannot create relation work.
- [x] Replay and SQLite restart add zero provider requests, model invocations,
  campaigns, runs, or writes on read.
- [x] Full checks, 668 control-plane tests, 30 Studio tests, production builds,
  and desktop/390 px Studio qualification pass.

## Non-goals

- a universal ontology of every real-world causal process;
- allowing an LLM confidence or embedding score to certify entity identity,
  causality, probability, or semantic novelty;
- treating a market price as the probability of a latent world state;
- recursively generating unbounded mechanism or relation work;
- live orders, credentials, signatures, approvals, transactions, or funds.

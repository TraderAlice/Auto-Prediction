# Falsifiable exploration hypotheses

Issue: https://github.com/luokerenx4/my-little-pony/issues/227

## Product proposition

Prediction markets are not merely prices attached to strings. They are venue
contracts whose traded payoffs are partial, socially selected observations of
world propositions. Durable arbitrage discovery therefore needs an Agent to
search for transformations of proposition structure, settlement scope and
payoff dependence—not only retrieve markets matching a predeclared claim.

The current V9 exploration episode preserves exact searches, effects, yield and
tokens, but it does not preserve the conjecture that made those actions a
coherent experiment. A query is retrieval mechanics, not a research intent.

## Decision

Introduce a V10, Agent-first hypothesis lifecycle inside prototype-guided
exploration:

- reconnaissance may happen before a hypothesis is opened;
- one active hypothesis at a time binds an exact prototype test or
  counter-scenario to a materially new variation, predicted role structure,
  supporting observations, falsifying observations and bounded neighborhoods;
- the Agent may revise the hypothesis in the same long loop;
- a terminal trailhead or bounded exhaustion requires at least one closed
  hypothesis with disposition `SUPPORTED`, `WEAKENED`, `FALSIFIED`, or
  `UNRESOLVED`;
- every lifecycle event is retained in the post-effect step ledger, so its
  effect, invocation and token lineage are exact;
- hypotheses route research memory only. They have no semantic, probability,
  certificate, campaign, execution or value-moving authority.

This is deliberately not a claim queue. A hypothesis describes an ontologically
material transformation to test and the observation that could defeat it. It
does not prescribe a famous subject or assert that a relation exists.

## Selection signals

`ADOPT` if one live Terra/high run can express, revise if needed, and close a
falsifiable hypothesis; the compiled episode preserves its exact lifecycle,
disposition, search yield and token lineage; and the Studio makes the research
intent legible without implying semantic truth.

`HOLD` if the lifecycle is durable but model behavior becomes materially more
prescriptive or expensive without improving experiment legibility.

`ABANDON` if fixed fields force claim-first behavior, if events cannot be bound
to exact effects, or if the hypothesis can silently grant semantic authority.

## Construction

1. Add open/revise/close tools with dynamic exact prototype-test handles.
2. Extend readiness so a closed hypothesis is required for either terminal.
3. Retain the lifecycle in content-addressed V10 step observations.
4. Compile final hypotheses and their revision history into experiment episodes.
5. Show material variation, predicted structure, falsifiers and disposition in
   durable Studio memory.
6. Qualify with focused and full tests, then run one explicit Terra/high live
   specimen before selection.

## Authority and cost

All lifecycle writes are local research memory. No provider call is made by a
projection, compiler or UI read. Only explicitly activated Agent runs incur
model cost. Live orders, signing, funds, credentials and automatic dispatch
remain structurally unavailable.

## Status

Selected `ADOPT`.

The focused lifecycle fixture retained an exact three-revision history
(`OPENED → REVISED → FALSIFIED`) and attributed 300 input tokens to the same
episode. Full qualification passed 108 control-plane files / 737 tests, 30
Studio tests and the production build.

The live Codex OAuth, Terra/high V10 specimen completed successfully under one
manual single-task campaign:

- run `9c2c88a5d558…`, episode `5f2a9e02fdd1…`;
- 10 successful invocations and 10 accepted effects with a complete ledger;
- 217,978 input, 1,795 output and 702 reasoning tokens;
- the Agent opened one hypothesis at effect 3, ran three searches, inspected
  one listing, and closed it `FALSIFIED` at effect 9 before the exhaustion
  terminal at effect 10;
- 38 raw hits became 16 qualified hits and zero role pairs;
- the retained falsifiers distinguish a present component-side listing from
  the absent aggregate-side contract instead of fabricating a relation;
- no result-repair call, semantic admission, automatic dispatch, external
  write or value-moving authority occurred.

Studio now shows the hypothesis's material variation, predicted structure,
prospective falsifier, disposition, revision count and exact effect span in
durable experiment memory. The live visual card remained legible beside the
historical V9 episode.

The main observed cost is 217,978 input tokens for one bounded negative
hypothesis. The next frontier is selection memory: compile hypothesis novelty,
disposition and yield/cost across episodes so future attention does not pay to
restate an already falsified conjecture under different prose.
